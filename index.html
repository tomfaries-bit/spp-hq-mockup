    Open questions. The mapping call decides the design.

    1. How do contractor invoices arrive? Email, PDF, Word, a spreadsheet?Decides whether step 1 can be read by software at all.
    2. Is each invoice already split by client, with date, task and hours on every line?Decides how much checking Ronnie still does by hand.
    3. Do hours go in as time entries under each contractor, or straight onto the invoice?Only time entries can be added from outside MyCase.
    4. Which MyCase plan does the firm pay for: Basic, Pro or Advanced?Pro allows Zapier. Advanced allows the API. Basic allows neither.
    5. Who is the firm's MyCase admin, and does Ronnie have the billing permission?That person has to request the connection from MyCase support.
    6. What rate does each client pay, and what is each contractor paid? Same rate card every month?Decides whether payouts in step 4 can be worked out on their own.
    7. "How much money came in": billed amounts, or payments received?Decides what the firm invoice in step 4 reads from MyCase.
    8. Where is the firm invoice built today, and who receives it in Detroit?Decides the format of step 4 and who signs off.

    Steps 2 and 3. Per client ZapierMyCase APIStays a click in MyCase

    Approved hours could post to MyCase as time entries under the right client, through Zapier's "Create Time Entry" step or the MyCase API. Time entries cannot be edited once posted, only deleted and redone. Creating the invoice is one click per client inside MyCase. Outside tools cannot do that step. If neither Zapier nor the API is allowed, the fallback is MyCase's own bulk-add screen.
